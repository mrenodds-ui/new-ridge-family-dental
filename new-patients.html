01 New patients welcome

Your first visit, unhurried.

No clipboard interrogation, no judgment about how long it’s been. Here’s exactly what to expect — from the first phone call to the moment you leave with a plan.

02 How it works

Four simple steps.

01

Request your appointment

Call the office or send the contact form with your preferred days. Either way, the office confirms your final time by phone — a real person, not a robot.

02

Complete registration

Fill out your new-patient forms before you arrive through the patient portal, so your visit starts on time and stays relaxed.

03

Meet Dr. Reno

Your first visit starts with a conversation — your history, your concerns, your goals. Then a thorough exam and low-dose digital X-rays.

04

Your plan, your pace

You leave with clear options and honest costs, written down. Nothing gets scheduled until you say so — no pressure, ever.
