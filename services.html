01 Services · New Ridge Family Dental

Complete care, one roof.

Comprehensive family dentistry in Wichita — from six-month cleanings to full smile makeovers, nearly everything happens right here with people you already know.

/ 01

Family & Preventive

Good dentistry is mostly prevention. Regular exams, gentle cleanings and low-dose digital X-rays let us catch small problems while they’re still small — for kids, parents and grandparents alike.

Every visit ends with a clear picture of where things stand and what, if anything, is worth watching.

  • Comprehensive exams & gentle cleanings
  • Low-dose digital X-rays
  • Sealants & fluoride for kids
  • Gum disease screening
/ 02

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic work should look like you — only rested, brighter and more confident. Veneers, bonding and smile design are planned around your face and your goals, never a one-size-fits-all look.

We start with a conversation about what you’d like to change, then show you realistic options and honest costs.

  • Porcelain veneers
  • Cosmetic bonding
  • Smile design & reshaping
/ 03

Teeth Whitening

Professional-grade whitening lifts years of coffee, tea and everyday stains safely — without the sensitivity that comes with drugstore strips. Choose a fast in-office treatment or custom trays you use at home.

Either way, we check your enamel first and set a shade goal that still looks natural.

  • In-office whitening, one visit
  • Custom take-home trays
  • Enamel & sensitivity check first
/ 04

Dental Implants

A missing tooth changes how you eat, speak and smile. Implants replace it with something that feels and works like the real thing — from a single tooth to a full arch.

Dr. Reno plans every case carefully with modern imaging, and crowns and bridges are finished right here in the office.

  • Single-tooth implants
  • Implant-supported bridges & full arch
  • Crowns & bridges
/ 05

Orthodontics

Straightening your teeth doesn’t have to mean a mouth full of metal. Discreet alignment options work quietly for teens and adults — most people won’t notice you’re in treatment.

We map the whole plan up front, so you know the timeline and the cost before you begin.

  • Clear aligner therapy
  • Options for teens & adults
  • Retainers & follow-up care
/ 06

Emergency Care

Toothache, chipped tooth or a knock-out on the ball field? Call (316) 722-6060 first thing in the morning — urgent concerns go straight to the front desk, and we hold same-day slots for exactly this.

If you’re not sure it’s an emergency, call anyway. A two-minute phone conversation is better than a weekend of worry.

  • Same-day urgent slots
  • Toothache, chip & trauma care
  • Call (316) 722-6060 first thing
