02 Meet the doctor

The doctor who knows your name.

Dr. Michael Reno founded New Ridge Family Dental on a simple belief: great dentistry starts with trust. A Wichita practitioner, he pairs advanced technology with uncompromising, conservative care — and the kind of attention only an owner-led practice can give.

When you call, you reach his team — not a call center. When you sit in the chair, you see him — not a rotation of associates. That continuity is the point.

His philosophy is conservative by default: treat what needs treating, watch what can safely wait, and explain the difference honestly. Advanced equipment is chosen with safety and efficiency in mind — better diagnostics, gentler visits, and less time in the chair for you.

04 The office

Right on North Ridge Road.

You’ll find us at 2135 North Ridge Rd, Suite 700 — easy to reach from anywhere in west Wichita, Andover, Maize or Goddard, with parking right outside the door. The office itself is calm and unhurried by design: warm light, quiet rooms, and none of the assembly-line feel of a big clinic.

The front-office team keeps everything running the way it should — appointments that start on time, reminders before you forget, and quick answers when you call. They’re the first voices you’ll hear, and they take that responsibility seriously.
