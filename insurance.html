01 Insurance & payment

Clear costs, no surprises.

Dental billing shouldn’t require a decoder ring. We explain your coverage in plain language and put every estimate in writing before treatment begins.

02 How coverage works here

Three things we do for you.

01

We verify your benefits

Before your visit, the front desk checks your plan and tells you what’s covered — so you walk in already knowing where you stand.

02

You get a written estimate

Before any treatment begins, you see the costs on paper — what insurance pays, what you pay, and what your options are.

03

We file the paperwork

Claims are handled for you, start to finish. If something comes back from the insurance company, we help you sort it out.

04 Common questions

Asked all the time.

/ 01

Do you take my insurance?

Most major plans are welcomed here. The fastest way to know for sure is to call (316) 722-6060 — the front desk will verify your specific benefits before your visit.

/ 02

What if I don’t have insurance?

You’re in good company — plenty of our patients are self-pay. Fees are clear, discussed up front, and put in writing before anything is scheduled.

/ 03

Can I get an estimate first?

Always. Every treatment plan comes with a written estimate showing what insurance covers and what you’d pay — before treatment begins, never after.

/ 04

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — monthly financing options are available for larger treatment plans. Ask the front desk and they’ll explain the choices in plain language.
